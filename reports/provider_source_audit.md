# CareRoute provider source audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-21  
**Scope:** All 12 rows in `data/raw/provider_source_inventory.csv`. Official source pages and documentation were reviewed as planning evidence. No provider profiles/records were collected, no APIs called, no downloads made, and no scraping performed. A source's official domain or public portal does **not** mean every record is verified, current, accessible, or licensed for reuse.

## Audit result at a glance

- The inventory has 12 candidate sources; all remain `candidate_not_collected`. No provider or source record has been marked verified by this audit.
- HFR/HPR and regulator directories are strongest for facility/professional identity or credential checks, but those do not prove service coverage, home visits, current availability, capacity, or fit for a patient's needs.
- Provider websites describe their own services and locations. Use them as claim sources; do not copy their content or assume public pages are licensed for republication.
- Of sources audited, **HFR/HPR have ABDM ecosystem API documentation**. That is not evidence of open anonymous access or a downloadable, licensed provider dataset. No generally reusable bulk export was confirmed for the other sources in this review.
- SHATAYU is a person-level geriatric caregiver directory. Its terms warn that information is candidate supplied and require users to independently verify identity/background/suitability. Its direct connection/caregiver placement model also needs policy review against CareRoute's non-employment-marketplace scope.

## Cross-source conclusions

### Provider categories and record grain

- **Organizations:** home-care companies and residential-care operators have an organization identity, potentially multiple brands, service offers and branches.
- **Individual professionals:** nurses, physiotherapists, doctors, psychologists and RCI-regulated professionals require separate qualification/registration checks and a lawful basis for public listing.
- **Physical facilities:** hospitals, clinics, rehab centres, labs and residences each need a location record. Facility registry membership does not establish home visits or a service-specific availability.
- **Caregivers:** SHATAYU entries concern individual people, not companies. They must not be collapsed into an agency record or described as nurses without evidence.
- **Service offers:** represent an organization-service-location combination. Keep source evidence, delivery mode, service area, date and verification state attached to claims.

The current facility dataset is a public-facility discovery seed with 166,462 records. It contains facility name/type, admin geography, address fragments, PIN, phone, point geometry/coordinates and source; it has no home-visit, nursing-provider, physiotherapy-provider, individual qualifications, current operating state or booking-availability field. The preprocessed dataset adds audit/standardization columns, not service evidence. The synthetic 1,000-row CSV is explicitly `SYNTHETIC_DEMO_ONLY`; do not merge it into a real-provider pilot.

### Reuse and access decision

At present, treat all source rows as **research candidates, not ingestion approvals**. The inventory’s “official website” means an official candidate page, not verified access permission. Before any records are stored or displayed, obtain written terms/API/export permission where needed, confirm data protection and source attribution, and decide how a public fact may be republished. An API being documented for ABDM ecosystem integrations is not authorization for CareRoute to call it or redistribute its data.
